## Reader outcome and selected presentation

**Loop → six article choices → reading → optional Ask conversation → the same reading.** Readers can change a loop’s future direction, find more worthwhile choices, return without losing their place, and deliberately share an article without their private context.

Keep the two-rule bulb, lowercase `edison` without a period, embedded Newsreader signature, existing interface/reading type roles and the selected Pulse-style cards. v10 is not a font migration or logo redesign. Later selected Pulse presentation supersedes older White Edition bans on its rounded cards. Library and Profile stay top right; subject tabs stay at the top, not in a new bottom navbar. The [Karri Saarinen reference criteria](KARRI_SAARINEN_DESIGN_REFERENCES.md) inform restrained hierarchy and careful continuity, not an endorsement. ## Implement the behavior, not the simulation

The artifact is an in-memory illustration. Its Desktop/Phone selector, review-state menu, known example topics, prepared transcript, manual completion controls and hash fixtures are **not production components**. Do not ship arbitrary prompt matching, fixture answers, simulated success, artificial completion timers or hard-coded reader context.

**Durability and isolation.** Persist loops, saved direction, article identity/version, Save/history and accepted request/response history under the actual owner, including the supported guest principal. Full multi-turn Ask persistence is selected; the prior latest-answer-only view is not sufficient. Preserve existing real latest answers in migration, but do not invent missing historical questions/turns. A conversation must include its actual question/answer/pending/failed history even when older turns fall outside the workspace's latest-120-request window. Restore the same conversation and pending operation after reload rather than duplicating generation. Draft/position recovery must be scoped by owner and article/version; document which fields are server-backed versus device-local. A local draft store must not leak across sign-out/accounts or imply unsupported cross-device sync. Saved server state and unsaved local drafts remain distinct. Audit existing cascade foreign keys: confirmed loop deletion must not delete retained article/chat/share identities indirectly.

**Request truth.** Stable operation identities and server state must handle rapid sends, repeated clicks, reload, uncertain network results and supported retries. Retry eligibility is backend-authoritative, not inferred from a generic error label. Reconcile an uncertain sending/pending request rather than resubmitting; one logical turn remains one turn, and duplicate prevention also runs server-side. Waiting for a request does not imply writing has started. A restored ready article or answer uses the saved result, not a new model call. Keep failure history and the actual allowance accounting. Tests can supply deterministic lifecycle outcomes without invoking a provider.

**Public sharing.** An owner URL does not confer public access. Implement a separate default-deny public token/version projection with only the intended title, deck, body and that version's evidence/metadata. Exclude instructions, `whyWritten`, personalization, owner identifiers not needed by the recipient, learning history and all chat turns on the server—not only with hidden buttons. Inspect rendered HTML, client/RSC/JSON payloads, prefetch and link-preview metadata, not only the visible page. Native sharing must share the public URL, not a private owner URL. Recipient navigation must not enter the owner's loops; use neutral Edison exploration. Owner-only edit/Ask/Save utilities cannot leak through the recipient route. A valid uncited article must also be shareable without inventing references or requiring a researched timestamp; test source-free and sourced demand articles, including supported guest ownership.

Create/recover the same share operation if a response is uncertain. Native share dismissal is not a failure or successful send; copying succeeds only after the API resolves. The mock’s displayed “Share sheet open” is a depicted review state, not evidence of an OS call and not a production success condition. Retain snapshot identity/version across loop edits/deletion; do not silently republish revised text under the guise of the same read. CTO should document implemented snapshot/correction/access behavior and any consequential unresolved choice through CoS rather than invent it. Deliver real server-generated title/deck link-preview metadata and verify it separately from the HTML recipient view. Use synthetic share content for acceptance; do not publicly share Michael’s data.
