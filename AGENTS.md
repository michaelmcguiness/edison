## Edison team context

For Edison project work, start with [the current milestone and owners](docs/operations/PROJECT_STATUS.md) and [the team guide](docs/operations/TEAM_GUIDE.md). Read your assigned role brief from the guide and only the source records needed for the task. A role title alone does not assign you another lead's work.

Use [the decision record](docs/operations/DECISIONS.md) to distinguish selected direction from proposals. Newer direct instructions from Michael take precedence. Preserve existing authorization; do not re-request it because an older handoff predates it.

Use the relevant Edison workflow in `.agents/skills/` for engineering delivery, design review, growth experiments, editorial review, or team coordination. If discovery has not refreshed, read its linked `SKILL.md` directly. Worktree agents must confirm that their source revision and shared records include the relevant current changes.

Each deliverable needs an owner, bounded scope, and evidence of completion. Distinguish proposed, implemented, verified, and live; attach the artifact/revision and verification limits. Preserve unrelated work in the shared checkout and agree file ownership or use isolated worktrees for parallel edits. Recurring corrections belong in the narrowest relevant role, skill, test, or decision record.
